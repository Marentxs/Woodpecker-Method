How this works

The Woodpecker method is a chess training system, developed by GMs Hans Tikkanen and Axel Smith

The point is to resolve the same batch of puzzles but faster everytime, which helps develop pattern recognition for tactical plays, improving accuracy and speed

Your progress is tracked each time you complete the full batch, and the rating is updated once you complete it 10 times

After completing the full batch 10 times your rating will be updated, and the weakest theme will change, with a new batch of exercises for the weakest theme
